respect at all times, thereby acting as a highly effective firewall against cascading logic 
failures.1

capability testing as outlined in frameworks like the OpenEvals guidebook.1 Model builders 
attempt to track the impact of changes in architectures, training recipes, and data mixtures 
through ablation studies.1 These experiments involve training a model under a specific setup 
and directly comparing its evaluation scores against a baseline to confirm whether 
performance has mathematically improved or degraded.1 By analyzing these ablations through 
intermediate and final checkpoints, developers use scaling laws to predict the future 
performance of much larger models.1 
However, LLM scoring is extraordinarily susceptible to superficial variables.1 Tokenization errors
